Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Overview

Zamzami Lab applications ("the App") are personal automation tools operated by Triza Zamzami. This policy explains how the App handles data obtained through Google APIs.

Data access

The App requests access to Google services (including Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, and related services) for the sole purpose of providing features to its owner. No data is collected from any other user.

Use and storage

  • Google user data is accessed only on the owner's instruction.
  • Credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored locally on the owner's private machine and are never shared with third parties.
  • The App does not sell, transfer, or use Google user data for advertising or any purpose unrelated to its core features.

Third parties

Google user data is not disclosed to any third party except as strictly necessary to provide the App's features (for example, Google's own API servers).

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the support email listed on the application's consent screen.
